# Share a real car or bike problem

**Describe a real problem you or your family had with a car or bike, in your own words.**

## What this is

I am testing a safety system that reads vehicle complaints. I want to see whether it understands Hindi, Hinglish and English. This is a research evaluation, not a repair service. I cannot fix your vehicle or contact the manufacturer.

## What to do

- Use the language you actually speak: Hindi, Hinglish, English, or a mix.
- Type your story, record your voice, or both. One is enough.
- Tell it in your own words. There is no right answer and no expected wording.
- Please do not include your name, phone number, or number plate.
- You can stop at any time before you submit.

## What is stored

Your story and recording are stored only on this laptop, in `data/real_hindi/`. They are not added to the code repository. I do not ask for identifying details, and I ask you not to include them. If you include them anyway, they will be stored with your story.

## AI processing

To evaluate the safety system, the text of your story (not the audio) may be sent to Meta's AI service (Muse Spark). Before it leaves this laptop, common phone-number, email, card, address and VIN patterns are replaced automatically. That pattern matching is not perfect.

The audio recording itself is not sent to Meta. If a transcript is needed, I type it myself on this laptop.

## Your choice

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop before you submit with no consequence. After you submit, you can ask me to delete your story. Because it is anonymous, I can only find it with the ID shown after you submit, so please save that ID. There is no payment or other benefit.

## Results

Only aggregate numbers will be shared. Your story will not be published. The evaluation may be inconclusive if too few stories are collected.

## After you submit

Save the ID shown on the next page. It is the only way I can find and delete an anonymous story.
