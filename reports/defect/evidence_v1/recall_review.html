Label and field boundaries
  • Record a reported symptom separately from the component the owner names. A propulsion complaint alone does not identify a fuel pump defect.
  • Preserve the owner’s denial, uncertainty and corrections. “No fire” is a denied fire report, never an affirmed fire event.
  • A suspected cause is not a workshop finding. State the reported source of an attribution; leave mechanical cause unknown when unsupported.
  • Copy dates, model years, part numbers and vehicle identifiers exactly. Keep both the earlier statement and an explicit correction when their relationship matters.
  • Review vehicle, part, symptom, failure mode, timing, negation and uncertainty. Unknown or absent fields can stay empty after explicit field review.
  • Use the frozen label definition only when supported by the report; use other / unclear for unrelated symptoms, ambiguous causes or insufficient evidence. Notes may retain ambiguity and competing interpretations.

Review independently. A second reviewer’s labels and model drafts must not be imported as your judgment. Disagreement is retained for separate adjudication.
